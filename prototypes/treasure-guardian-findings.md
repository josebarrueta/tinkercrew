## Implementation dry run

The prototype currently supports the full intended loop in memory:

- Crew setup with Explorer, Inventor, and Hacker tracks
- Shared rotating roles
- Story briefing and an open-ended creative build confirmation
- Simulated Distance, Buttons, Pixels, and Buzzer
- Editable warning and intruder thresholds
- Three progressive hints
- Seven automatic behavior checks
- Required remix and reflection before completion
- Free Build prompt generation
- Visible state and action history after every interaction

Potential questions to watch in the family session:

1. Seven automatic checks may be too many for the youngest participant to track.
2. “Track” and “threshold” may need more child-friendly language.
3. Preset remixes may not feel sufficiently authored by the crew.
4. Role rotation may be disruptive if it occurs too frequently.
5. A shared mouse/trackpad may allow one participant to dominate despite distinct roles.
